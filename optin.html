My Tech, LLC — WhatsApp Messaging Program

My Tech, LLC ("My Tech") is a wholesale distributor of computer hardware and accessories based in Doral, FL. This page describes our WhatsApp messaging program and how customers and vendors can opt in.

What messages will I receive?

By opting in, you agree to receive transactional WhatsApp messages from My Tech related to your business relationship with us, including:

  • Order confirmations, invoices, estimates, and purchase order updates
  • Shipment, delivery, and backorder status
  • Responses to your own requests (e.g. asking for a specific invoice, checking item availability or pricing)

Message frequency is typically a few messages per transaction. This program is not used for marketing or promotional messages, and numbers are only added with the customer's or vendor's direct consent.

Consent statement: By providing your phone number to My Tech and requesting to be contacted via WhatsApp, you consent to receive the transactional messages described above at that number. Message and data rates may apply.

How do I opt in?

Provide your phone number directly to your My Tech sales or account representative and confirm you would like to receive order and account updates via WhatsApp.

How do I opt out?

Reply STOP at any time to any message to be removed from this messaging program. You may also contact your My Tech representative directly to opt out.
