My Tech, LLC — WhatsApp Messaging Consent

My Tech, LLC is a wholesale distributor of computer hardware and accessories based in Doral, FL. Consent to receive WhatsApp messages is collected verbally by our sales and account representatives during normal business conversations with customers and vendors.

Verbal consent script used by our team

Before adding a customer or vendor's number to our WhatsApp messaging program, our representative reads the following to them and confirms agreement. The representative directs the customer to this page to review the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions before consent is given:

"Would you like to receive your order updates via WhatsApp? This would include invoices, estimates, and purchase order or shipment status at this phone number. You can review our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions at ykroitoro.github.io/mytech-whatsapp-optin before agreeing. Message frequency varies by transaction, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving these messages. Do we have your permission to add you?"

The customer or vendor's verbal "yes" — given after being directed to review the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions linked below — is recorded by the representative as consent before any number is added to the messaging program.

What messages will I receive?

By providing your mobile phone number to My Tech, LLC and verbally agreeing to the script above, you expressly consent to receive transactional WhatsApp messages from My Tech, LLC at the number provided, including invoices, estimates, purchase order updates, and shipment/backorder status. Message frequency varies by transaction. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for assistance. This number is not used for marketing or promotional messages. No mobile information, including phone numbers and messaging consent, will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from My Tech, LLC at any time to immediately stop receiving further messages. You may also contact your My Tech, LLC representative directly.
