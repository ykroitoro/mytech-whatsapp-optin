My Tech, LLC — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Tech, LLC ("we," "us," "our"), a wholesale distributor of computer hardware and accessories based in Doral, FL, collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS and WhatsApp messaging program ("Program"), as well as our general business operations.

1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to the Program, we collect your mobile phone number and associate it with your existing customer or vendor account. We also retain the content of messages exchanged through the Program, such as document requests or item inquiries, in order to respond to you and maintain accurate business records.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to operate the Program: sending transactional messages (invoices, estimates, purchase order updates, shipment/backorder status) and responding to requests you send us. We do not use your phone number or message content for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to your business relationship with us.

3. How We Share Your Information

No mobile information, including phone numbers and messaging consent, will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We use Twilio, Inc. solely as our messaging platform provider to deliver and receive messages on our behalf; Twilio processes this data only to provide that service, under its own privacy policy, and not for its own marketing. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message frequency varies by transaction. Message and data rates may apply, as determined by your mobile carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

5. Your Choices

You may opt out of the Program at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us directly (details below).

6. Data Retention

We retain phone numbers and message records for as long as needed to maintain accurate business and customer records, or until you request deletion.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through the Program from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued participation in the Program after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
